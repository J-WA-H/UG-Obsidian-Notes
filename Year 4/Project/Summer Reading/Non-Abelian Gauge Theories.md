The previous model can be generalised to an invariance under some arbitrary group $G$ of global gauge transformations. These act on an $n$-component scalar field $\phi_{i}$ though the action of a matrix representation of this group,
$$\phi_{i}\to \phi'_{i}=D_{ij}(g)\phi_{j} \tag{2.1}$$where $D(g)$ is an $n\times n$ matrix and $g\in G$.
Elements of $G$ can be written as
$$g=\exp(-i\omega_{a}L^{a})\tag{2.2} $$where $\omega_{a}$ are arbitrary real numbers and $L^{a}$ are the $N$ group generators, which satisfy
$$\left[L^a,L^b\right]=-if_{abc}L^c\tag{2.3} $$
where $f_{abc}$ are structure constants of $G$.

A matrix representation of arbitrary dimension $n$ can be generated by finding a set of $n\times n$ matrices $T^{a}$ satisfying the same commutation relations.
We can take $G$ as a group of matrices by taking any one-to-one representation of $G$ with basis of Hermitian generators $\{ L^{a} \}$.

For a compact Lie group $G$, the generators can be normalised by
$$\mathrm{Tr}(T^aT^b)=\delta_{ab}.\tag{2.4} $$
The correspondence between the two generators is taken as $T^a=D(L^a)$.

By choosing $\varphi=\{ \phi_{i} \}$, $\bra{0}\varphi \ket{0}=\varphi_{0}$. The elements of $G$ that leave $\varphi_{0}$ unchanged form a group $H$,
$$H=\{ g\in G\;|\;D(g)\varphi_{0}=\varphi_{0} \}\tag{2.5}.$$
The generators $t^{\alpha}$ of $H$ annihilate $\varphi$ s.t. $t^\alpha \varphi_{0}=0$.
These are chosen s.t. $t^{\alpha}\subset T^{a}$. The $t^{\alpha}$ are known as unbroken generators of $G$ and the remaining $T^a$s are the broken generators.

The vacuum defined by $\bra{0}\varphi\ket{0}=\varphi_{0}$ is one of an infinite number of equivalent vacuums with $\bra{0}\varphi \ket{0}=D(g)\phi_{0}$. This can be used to define the vacuum manifold $\mathcal{M}$,
$$\mathcal{M}=G/H.\tag{2.6} $$
The topology of $\mathcal{M}$ determines the nature of topological defects.

Introduce the gauge fields $A^a_{\mu}$ which is associated with matrices in the Lie algebra of $G$, $\mathcal{A}_{\mu}=A^a_\mu L^{a}$. This gives the non-abelian generalisation of the Higgs model
$$\mathcal{L}=\frac{1}{2}\mathcal{D}_{\mu}\varphi_{i}\mathcal{D}^{\mu}\varphi_{i}-V(\varphi)-\frac{1}{4}F^{a}_{\mu \nu}F^{a\mu \nu} \tag{2.7} $$
where $F^{a}_{\mu \nu}=\partial_{\mu}A^{a}_{\nu}-\partial_{\nu}A^{a}_{\mu}+ef_{abc}A^{b}_{\mu}A^{c}_{\nu}$ and $\mathcal{D}_{\mu}=\partial_{\mu}-ieA^{a}_{\mu}T^{a}$.

The vector fields associated with broken generators require non-zero mass, while $H$ remains massless.
