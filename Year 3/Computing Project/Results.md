#### Lay Summary
Everyone was taught in school that everything is made out of atoms - but have you considered what atoms are made of. Atoms are actually made up of smaller particles called protons, neutrons, and electrons. This can be seen similar to how the Earth orbits the Sun, the electrons orbit the nucleus of the atom, which is made of protons and neutrons. These protons and neutrons are really interesting as they are made up of even smaller particles called quarks.
\\\\These quarks can form many particles - not just protons and neutrons. One type of particle quarks make is quarkonium. This is where two quarks orbit each other - like the electron orbiting the nucleus in an atom. One of these quarks has to be an anti-quark - this is a quark that is exactly the same as a normal quark but it has the opposite charge.
\\\\This project studies these quarkonium systems by calculating their mass - how much they appear to weigh. At first thought you may think that the mass of quarkonium is just the mass of the quarks. This is wrong. These quarks are orbiting each other so there is energy keeping them together. Due to Einstein's famous equation, $E=mc^2$, energy and mass can be measured as the same thing.
