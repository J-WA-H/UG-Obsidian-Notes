The conduction band (CB) is the lowest unoccupied band at $T=0\text{ K}$ with minimal energy $E_C$.
The valance band (VB) is the highest occupied band with maximum energy $E_{V}$.

$E_g$ is the band gap, the energy difference between the two bands.
$$E_{g}=E_{C}-E_{V}.$$
For a semiconductor, $E_{g}\approx1-3\text{ eV}$.
