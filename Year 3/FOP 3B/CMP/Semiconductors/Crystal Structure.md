Consider the crystal structure of the [semiconductor](Band%20Gaps%20in%20Semiconductors.md) silicon - it is a diamond cubic $\equiv$ 2 fcc sub lattices shifted by $\frac{1}{4}[111]$.
An fcc lattice has an atom at each corner, contributing $\frac{1}{8}$ of it's volume to the unit cell, and an atom in the centre of each face, each contributing $\frac{1}{2}$ to its unit cell,
![[Pasted image 20250212160914.png]]
fcc lattices have 4 atoms per unit cell.

For the diamond cubic, it is a combination of 2 fcc sublattices shifted by the vector $\frac{1}{4}[111]$.

If all atoms are the same, this shows tetrahedral bonding.
