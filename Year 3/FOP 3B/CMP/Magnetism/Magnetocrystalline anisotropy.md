In some materials it is easier to magnetise in some directions than others due to crystal field effects from [spin orbit](Angular%20Momentum%20in%20QM.md) coupling.
Consider metallic cobalt
![[Pasted image 20250130145555.png]]
The $c$ axis is known as the easy axis. 
![[Pasted image 20250130145708.png]]
For a B field parallel to the easy axis, a smaller B field is needed to achieve the same magnetisation as a B field orthogonal to the easy axis, known as the hard axis.

The magnetocrystalline energy density is
$$\varepsilon=K_{1}\sin^2\theta+K_{2}\sin^4\theta$$
where $K_{1},K_{2}$ are positive constants and $\theta$ is the angle of the B field with respect to the easy axis.
