The coherence length, $\xi$, is given by
$$\xi(T)=\left( \frac{\hbar^2}{2m|a(T)|} \right)^{1/2}=\left( \frac{\hbar^2}{2m\dot{a}|T-T_{c}|} \right)^{1/2}.$$
$\xi$ represents the [[Cooper Pairs]] electron density.
![[Pasted image 20250227162709.png]]
The right Cooper pair is unstable as superconducting electrons cannot exist in the normal state.
All Cooper pairs must be within the superconducting state.
