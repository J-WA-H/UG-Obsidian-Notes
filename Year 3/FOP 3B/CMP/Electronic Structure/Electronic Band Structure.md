We can extend the Energy relation for the [[Free-Electron Theory]] to adjacent Brillouin zone.
![[Pasted image 20250117182713.png]]
Due to the periodic nature of crystals, there are periodic copies of each E-k diagram at each reciprocal point $G$. Each E-k diagram contributes a single band to a given Brillouin zone. Intersections of E-k diagrams at the zone boundaries create [band gaps](Nearly-Free%20Electron%20Theory.md).

The extended zone scheme represents an E-k diagram where all $k$ values are represented.
![[Pasted image 20250117184108.png]]
The reduced zone scheme only gives k values within the first zone.
