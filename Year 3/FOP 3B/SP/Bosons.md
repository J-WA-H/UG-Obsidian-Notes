We can calculate the energy for bosons using the [[Bose-Einstein Distribution]].
$$U=\int_{0}^{\infty}\hbar ck \frac{1}{8} \frac{4\pi k^2dk}{\left( \frac{\pi}{a} \right)^3} \frac{1}{e^{\beta(E(k)-\mu)}-1}.$$
Using the integral
$$\int_{0}^{\infty} \frac{x^3}{e^{x}-1}dx=\frac{8\pi^4}{15}$$
we get an energy density of
$$\frac{U}{V}=\frac{\pi^2}{30} \frac{(K_{B}T)^4}{(\hbar c)^3}.$$
