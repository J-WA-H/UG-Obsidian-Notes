Consider two [spin-1/2](Spin.md) particles each with its own Hilbert space, $\mathcal{H}^{(1)}=\text{span}\{\ket{\alpha}_{1},\ket{\beta}_{1}\}$ and $\mathcal{H}^{(2)}=\text{span}\{\ket{\alpha}_{2},\ket{\beta}_{2}\}$.
The total spin will be given by $\hat{S}=\hat{S}^{(1)}+\hat{S}^{(2)}$ and the $z$ component $\hat{S}_{z}=\hat{S}_{z}^{(1)}+\hat{S}_{z}^{(2)}$.
To understand the combined system we take the Tensor product of the two Hilbert spaces
$$\mathcal{H}^{(1)}\otimes \mathcal{H}^{(2)}=\text{span}\{\ket{\alpha}_{1}\ket{\alpha}_{2},\ket{\alpha}_{1}\ket{\beta}_{2},\ket{\beta}_{1}\ket{\alpha}_{2},\ket{\beta}_{1}\ket{\beta}_{2}\}.$$
The total spin and z component spin for this system can be characterised by the quantum numbers:
$$|s^{(1)}-s^{(2)}|\leq S \leq s^{(1)}+s^{(2)}$$
$$-S\leq M\leq S.$$
$$\hat{S}^2\ket{S,M} =S(S+1)\hbar^2\ket{S,M} $$
$$\hat{S}_{z}\ket{S,M}=M\hbar \ket{S,M}.$$
The four spin eigenstates are
$$\ket{S=0,M=0}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(\ket{\alpha}_{1}\ket{\beta}_{2}-\ket{\beta}_{1}\ket{\alpha}_{2})$$
$$\ket{S=1,M=0}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{ 2 }}(\ket{\alpha}_{1}\ket{\beta}_{2}+\ket{\beta}_{1}\ket{\alpha}_{2})$$
$$\ket{S=1,M=-1}=\ket{\beta}_{1}\ket{\beta}_{2}$$
$$\ket{S=1,M=1}=\ket{\alpha}_{1}\ket{\alpha}_{2}.$$
