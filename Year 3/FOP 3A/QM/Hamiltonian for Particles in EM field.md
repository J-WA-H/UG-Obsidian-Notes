Classically, a charged particle experiences a [Lorentz force](Intro%20and%20EMF.md), $\underline{F}=q(\underline{E}+\underline{v}\times \underline{B})$. We can relate $\underline{E}$ and $\underline{B}$ to a scalar and a vector potential:
$$\underline{E}=-\underline{\nabla}\phi(r)+\frac{\partial}{\partial t}\underline{A}(\underline{r},t);\;\underline{B}=\underline{\nabla}\times \underline{A}(\underline{r},t)$$
The Hamiltonian for a charged particle in an EM field is given by
$$\hat{H}=\frac{1}{2m}[-i\hbar \underline{\nabla}-q\underline{A}(\underline{r},t)]^2+q\phi(\underline{r})$$
We measure $\underline{E}$ and $\underline{B}$ instead of $\phi$, $\underline{E}$, $\underline{B}$.
