Consider the decay of a particle with mass $m$ and 4-momentum $p^{\mu}$ into two particles of mass $m_{A}$ and $m_{B}$ and momenta $p^{\mu}_{A}$ and $p_{B}^{\mu}$ respectively.
![[Pasted image 20241116133230.png]]
Due to conservation of momentum $p^{\mu}=p^{\mu}_{A}+p^{\mu}_{B}$.
Calculate [Minkowski norm](Invariants.md)
$$p_{\mu}p^{\mu}=(p_{A}+p_{B})_{\mu}(p_{A}+p_{B})^{\mu}=p_{A\mu}p_{A}^{\mu}+p_{B\mu}p_{B}^{\mu}+2p_{A}p_{B}$$
$$=m_{A}^2+m_{B}^2+2p_{A}p_{B}=m^2.$$
From this we know
$$2p_{A}p_{B}=m^2-m_{A}^2-m_{B}^2.$$
Moving into the rest frame of the decaying particle, $p^\mu=(m,\underline{0})$.
![[Pasted image 20241116134301.png]]
$$\underline{p}_{A}+\underline{p}_{B}=\underline{0}\implies \theta=\pi^{r}.$$
Take $m_{A}=m_{B}$:
$$2p_{A}p_{B}=2(E_{A}E_{B}-\lvert \underline{p}_{A} \rvert\lvert \underline{p}_{B} \rvert \cos \theta  )=m^2-m_{A}^2-m_{B^2}$$
$$\implies 2(E_{A}^2+\lvert \underline{p}_{A} \rvert^2 )=m-2m_{A}^2$$
$$\lvert \underline{p}_{A} \rvert=\frac{\sqrt{ m^2-4m_{A}^2 }}{2}. $$
As momentum must be real, we get the condition that $m\geq{2}m_{A}$ which shows the conservation of mass/energy in decay.
