We have only been considering isobaric decay processes - preserving the number of nucleons $A$, like [[Beta decay]].

A-changing decay are processes where the number of nucleons change, radiated either by the EM or strong force.

Nucleon emission is the decay where a nucleon is released.
$${}^{A}_ZX\to {}^{A-1}_{Z-1}Y+p^{+}.$$
The energy balance of this process is
$$M(A,Z)>M(A-1,Z-1)+M(1,1).$$
For the emission of a neutron:
$${}^{A}_{Z}X\to {}^{A-1}_{Z}Y+n$$
$$M(A,Z)>M(A-1,Z-1)+M(1,0).$$
Alpha emission is the emission of an alpha particle, a ${}^4_{2}He$ nucleus.
$${}^{A}_{Z}X\to {}^{A-4}_{Z-2}Y+{}^{4}_{2}He$$
$$M(A,Z)>M(A-4,Z-2)+M(4,2).$$
