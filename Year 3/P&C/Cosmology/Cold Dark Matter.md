As there are no other candidates in the standard model other than [[Hot Dark Matter]], we have to look outside the standard model for a better theory. One example can be found from supersymmetry.

Supersymmetry states that each fermion has a bosonic partner and bosons have a fermionic partner.

For bosonic fermion partners
- Leptons $\to$ sleptons
- Neutrinos $\to$ sneutrinos
- Quarks $\to$ squarks

The fermionic partners for bosons are
- Photons $\to$ Photino
- Higgs $\to$ Higgsino
- Graviton $\to$ Gravitino
- W $\to$ Wino.
The photino, higgsino and gravitino are known as weakly interacting massive particles (WIMPs).

WIMPs are produced turn non-relativistic as they are produced so they have small energy which is why this theory is cold dark matter.

WIMPs have a density parameter of $\Omega_{WIMP_{0}}\approx 0.25$ which matches the observed dark matter density parameter - so WIMPs are very good candidates. However, they have yet to be detected.
