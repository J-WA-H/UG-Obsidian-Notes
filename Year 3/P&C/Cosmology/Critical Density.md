Start by considering the [[Friedmann Equation]]:
$$3H^2(t)=8\pi G\rho(t)-\frac{3kc^2}{a^2}$$
$$\frac{3H^2}{8\pi G}=\rho(t)-\frac{3kc^2}{8\pi Ga^2}.$$
The left hand side is known as the critical density
$$\rho_{crit}(t)=\frac{3H^2(t)}{8\pi G}.$$
The Friedmann equation shows that the critical density describes the density of matter in the universe and the geometry of the universe, as it depends on $k$.
$\rho_{crit}$ can be used to give an estimate of the density of the universe
$$\rho_{crit}\approx 10^{-26}\text{ kg m}^{-3}\approx 1.5\times 10^{11} \text{ M}_{\odot}\text{Mpc}^{-3}\approx 6\text{ m}_{p}\text{ m}^{-3}.$$
The density parameter gives the share of matter given by a certain matter species. For the $i^{th}$ species it is given by
$$\Omega_{i}(a)=\frac{\rho_{i}(a)}{\rho_{crit}(a)}.$$
As $\Omega$ is a fraction,
$$0\leq \Omega_{i}\leq 1.$$
Using the definition of critical density gives
$$\Omega_{i}(a)=\frac{8\pi G\rho_{i}(a)}{3H^2(a)}.$$
This can be used to simplify the Friedmann equation:
Using the densities of the three [matter species](Acceleration%20Equation.md), $\rho(a)=\rho_{R}(a)+\rho_{M}(a)+\rho_{\Lambda}(a)$:
$$\frac{H^2}{H_{0}^2}=\frac{1}{H_{0}^2}\left[  \frac{8\pi G}{3}\rho_{R_{0}}a^{-4}+ \frac{8\pi G}{3}\rho_{M_{0}}a^{-3}+\frac{8\pi G}{3}\rho_{\Lambda_{0}} \right]-\frac{kc^2}{a^2H_{0}^2}$$
$$=\Omega_{R_{0}}a^{-4}+\Omega_{M_{0}}a^{-3}+\Omega_{\Lambda_{0}}-\frac{kc^2}{a^2H_{0}^2}.$$
Evaluate this equation today:
$$1=\Omega_{R_{0}}+\Omega_{M_{0}}+\Omega_{\Lambda_{0}}-\frac{kc^2}{H_{0}^2}$$
$$\implies kc^2=H_{0}^2(\Omega_{R_{0}}+\Omega_{M_{0}}+\Omega_{\Lambda_{0}}-1)$$
$$\therefore H^2(t)=H_{0}^2[\Omega_{R_{0}}a^{-4}+\Omega_{M_{0}}a^{-3}+\Omega_{\Lambda_{0}}-(\Omega _{R_{0}}+\Omega_{M_{0}}+\Omega_{\Lambda_{0}}-1)a^{-2}].$$
