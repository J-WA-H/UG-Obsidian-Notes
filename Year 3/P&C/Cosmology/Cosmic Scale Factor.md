Due to the [expansion](Expanding%20Universe.md) of the universe, the physical separation of galaxies increases over time, described by
$$r(t)=a(t)r_{0}$$
where $r_{0}$ is the present separation and $a(t)$ is the scale factor, where today at $t=t_{0}$, $a(t_{0})=1$ and $a(0)=0$.

![[Pasted image 20250116123232.png]]

A comoving coordinate is a coordinate system that expands with time, so the tick marks move together with the galaxies.

![[Pasted image 20250116160441.png]]

As $r_{0}$ is independent of time, it is known as the comoving distance.

The physical density of the universe is given by 
$$\rho(t)=\frac{\rho_{0}}{a^3(t)}$$
where
$$\rho_{0}=\frac{M}{r_{0}^{3}}.$$
