For an accurate [cosmological model](Cosmological%20Models.md), we need contributions from matter, radiation and the cosmological constant, $\Omega_{R_{0}}=9.27\times10^{-5}$, $\Omega_{M_{0}}=0.315$, $\Omega_{\Lambda_{0}}=0.685$. As the universe is mostly flat, the [Friedmann equation](Critical%20Density.md) becomes 
$$\frac{H^2}{H_{0}^2}=\Omega_{R_{0}}a^{-4}+\Omega_{M_{0}}a^{-3}+\Omega_{\Lambda_{0}}.$$
This can be used to find the time dependence of $\Omega_{i}$. Consider $\Omega_{R}$:
$$\Omega_{R}(a)=\frac{8\pi G\rho_{R}(a)}{3H^2}=\frac{8\pi G\rho_{R_{0}}a^{-4}}{3H_{0}^2[\Omega_{R_{0}}a^{-4}+\Omega_{M_{0}}a^{-3}+\Omega_{\Lambda_{0}}]}$$
$$=\frac{\Omega_{R_{0}}a^{-4}}{\Omega_{R_{0}}a^{-4}+\Omega_{M_{0}}a^{-3}+\Omega_{\Lambda_{0}}}.$$
This also gives
$$\Omega_{M}(a)=\frac{\Omega_{M_{0}}a^{-3}}{\Omega_{R_{0}}a^{-4}+\Omega_{M_{0}}a^{-3}+\Omega_{\Lambda_{0}}}$$
$$\Omega_{\Lambda}(a)=\frac{\Omega_{\Lambda_{0}}}{\Omega_{R_{0}}a^{-4}+\Omega_{M_{0}}a^{-3}+\Omega_{\Lambda_{0}}}.$$
In a flat universe, the density parameter of matter species $i$ is the fraction of the total density contributed by that species.

This divides the universe into three eras, radiation dominated, matter dominated, and $\Lambda$ dominated.
The boundary between the radiation and matter dominated eras is the matter-radiation equality.
$$\rho_{M}(a_{eq})=\rho_{R}(a_{eq})$$
$$\rho_{M_{0}}a_{eq}^{-3}=\rho_{R_{0}}a^{-4}_{eq}$$
$$\implies a_{eq}=\frac{\rho_{R_{0}}}{\rho_{M_{0}}}=\frac{\Omega_{R_{0}}}{\Omega_{M_{0}}}.$$
