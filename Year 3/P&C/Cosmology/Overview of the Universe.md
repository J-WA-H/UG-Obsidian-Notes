The universe is isotropic - it looks the same in every direction.
The universe is homogeneous - it looks the same at every point.

On large scales, the universe is isotropic and homogeneous.

Using a current value of Hubble's constant is $H_{0}=70\text{ km s}^{-1}\text{Mpc}^{-1}$, we get an estimate of the age of the universe as
$$t_{H}=\frac{1}{H_{0}}\approx 14 \text{ billion years}.$$
$t_H$ is the Hubble time which gives a rough but accurate estimate for the age of the universe.
