Consider the first law of thermodynamics
$$d\varepsilon=dQ-PdV.$$
As there is no heat source for the universe, $dQ=0$.
$$\therefore d\varepsilon=-PdV.$$
Take $d\varepsilon=\rho c^2V\propto \rho c^2a^3$ where $V$ is the volume of the universe.
$$\frac{d\varepsilon}{dt}=\dot{\rho}c^2a^3+\rho c^23a^2\dot{a}$$
$$=\dot{\rho}c^2a^3+\rho c^23a^3\left( \frac{\dot{a}}{a} \right)$$
$$=c^2a^3\left[ \dot{\rho}+3\left( \frac{\dot{a}}{a} \right)\rho \right].$$
$$-P\frac{dV}{dt}=-P \frac{da^3}{dt}=-3Pa^3 \frac{\dot{a}}{a}.$$
Using the 1st law:
$$c^2a^3\left[ \dot{\rho}+3 \frac{\dot{a}}{a}\rho \right]=-3Pa^3 \frac{\dot{a}}{a}$$
$$\implies \dot{\rho}+3 \frac{\dot{a}}{a}\left( \rho+\frac{P}{c^2} \right)=0.$$
This is the fluid equation.
