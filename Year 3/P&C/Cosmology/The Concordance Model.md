The concordance ($\Lambda CDM$) model is the current [cosmological model](Cosmological%20Models.md), with [density parameters](Critical%20Density.md),
$$\Omega_{k_{0}}=0.001\pm 0.002$$
so the universe is very close to being flat.
$$H_{0}=67.4\pm 0.5\text{ km s}^{-1}\text{Mpc}^{-1}$$
$$T_{CMB}=2.726\text{ K}$$
$$\Omega_{b_{0}}=0.0494\pm 0.0007$$
$$\Omega_{M_{0}}=0.315 \pm 0.007,$$
this is the combination of baryonic and [[Cold Dark Matter]].
$$\Omega_{\Lambda_{0}}=0.685\pm 0.007$$
$$T_{\nu}=1.956\text{ K}$$
$$\sum_{i=1}^{3}m_{\nu_{i}}<0.12\text{ eV/c}^2.$$
This shows there is a small amount of [[Hot Dark Matter]] that contributes to dark matter, but CDM dominates.

This fits well with nearly all cosmological observations so far but we don't know the nature of dark matter.
We also don't the nature of dark energy either and there is a disagreement about the nature of $H_{0}$ so there are flaws with this model.

Example: At inflation, $a=10^{-27}$, $\Omega_{R_{0}}=8\times 10^{-3}$ and $\Omega_{\Lambda_{0}}=0.7$, calculate the ratio of the $\Lambda$ and radiation densities,
$$\frac{\rho_{\Lambda}}{\rho_{R}}=\frac{\rho_{\Lambda_{0}}}{\rho_{R_{0}}}a^{4}=\frac{\Omega_{\Lambda_{0}}}{\Omega_{R_{0}}}a^{4}\approx 9\times 10^{-105}$$
This massive ratio is the coincidence problem.
