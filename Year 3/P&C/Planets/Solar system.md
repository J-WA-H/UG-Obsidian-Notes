### Definitions for a solar system
- The extent of the magnetic field from the host star
- The extent of the gravitation field from the host star

### Properties
- Isolated system
- Mass is primarily the sun
- Angular momentum is primarily the planetary orbits
- All planets orbit in an approximate plane
- All planets and most planets have prograde orbits and spins

### Terrestrial planets
#### Mercury
- No atmosphere
- Approx same density as Earth $\implies$ no mantle
#### Venus
- Same mass as Earth
- $CO_2$ rich atmosphere
#### Earth
- Crust made of tectonic plates
- Has a moon ~ 2% mass of Earth
#### Mars
- Thin $CO_2$ atmosphere
- Has season like Earth due to tilt in the rotational axis
### Giant Planets
#### Jupiter
- Largest planet in the solar system
- Strong magnetic field generating auroras at the poles
- Has about 100 moons
##### Io
- Sulphur rich
##### Europa
- Evidence of liquid water
#### Saturn
- Ring made from ice
- Thickness of about 10 - 100m
#### Uranus
- About $14M_E$
- Has ring system
#### Neptune
- hydrogen, helium atmosphere
- Has ring system
