Radiation pressure pushes bodies away from the sun. If a fraction $Q$ of the photon flux $S$ is absorbed then a particle with radius $a$ experiences a force $\frac{QS\pi a^2}{c}$. At a distance $r$ from the sun
$$F_{rad}=\frac{QL_{\odot}a^2}{4cr^2}.$$
Like gravity, it is an inverse square force so the ratio of the attractive and repulsive force is independent of $r$.

##### Poynting-Robertson effect
Aberration of light for particles around $1\text{ cm}$ in size. Greater for higher $\frac{v}{c}$ so reduces eccentricity. There is a loss of angular momentum so particles spiral inwards
$$F_{PR}=\frac{v}{c} \frac{QL_{\odot}a^2}{4cr^2}.$$
##### Yarkowsky effect
Important for rotating bodies around $1\text{ cm} - 10\text{ km}$ in size. Prograde spin leads to expanded orbits whereas Yarkowsky effect shrinks the orbits of the bodies with retrograde spin.
