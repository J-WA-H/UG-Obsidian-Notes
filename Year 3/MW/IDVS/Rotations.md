Rotations are equivalent to [[Coordinate Transforms]].
The rotation matrices about each axis in the basis $\{\underline{e}_{1},\underline{e}_{2},\underline{e}_{3}\}$ about an angle $\phi$ is given by
$$\underline{\underline{R}}_{1}(\phi)=\begin{bmatrix}
1 & 0 & 0 \\
0 & \cos \phi & \sin \phi \\
0 &-\sin \phi & \cos \phi
\end{bmatrix}$$
$$\underline{\underline{R}}_{2}(\phi)=\begin{bmatrix}
\cos \phi & 0 & \sin \phi \\
0 & 1 & 0 \\
-\sin \phi & 0 & \cos \phi
\end{bmatrix}$$
$$\underline{\underline{R}}_{3}(\phi)=\begin{bmatrix}
\cos \phi & \sin \phi & 0 \\
-\sin \phi & \cos \phi & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 1
\end{bmatrix}.$$
