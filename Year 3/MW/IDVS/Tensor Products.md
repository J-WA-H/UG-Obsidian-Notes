Let $V$ be an $n$ dimensional [vector space](Vector%20Spaces.md) with [basis](Basis%20of%20Vectors.md) $E=\{\underline{e}_{i}\}$.
Let $W$ be an $m$ dimensional vector space with basis $G=\{\underline{g}_j\}$.

Define $X$ as a third vector space which is the tensor product of $V$ and $W$,
$$X=V\otimes W.$$
The basis for $X$ is made out of pairs of elements from each vector space
$$E\otimes G=\{E\otimes G\}.$$
The dimension of $X$ is the product of the dimensions of $V$ and $W$.

Example:
If $V\subset \mathbb{R}$ and $W \subset \mathbb{R}$ then $X\subset \mathbb{R}^2$.
