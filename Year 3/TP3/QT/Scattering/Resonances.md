Looking at the $\ell^{th}$ partial wave [cross section](Phase%20Shifts.md):
$$\sigma_{\ell}=\frac{4\pi}{k^2}(2\ell+1) \frac{1}{1+\cot(\delta_{\ell})}.$$
As $\cot(\delta_{\ell})\to 0$. we get resonance in the $\ell^{th}$ partial wave in scattering related to a particle with spin $\ell$.
The maximum of the cross-section is a sharp peak which has a clear experimental signature.

At resonance energy $E_R$:
$$\cot(\delta_{\ell})=\frac{{E_{R}-E}}{\Gamma\left( E \right)/2}$$
where $\Gamma$ is the full width-half maximum at $E_{R}$.

This gives the Breit-Wigner resonance formula:
$$\sigma_{\ell}=\frac{4\pi}{k^2} \frac{(2\ell+1)\Gamma^2(E_{R})/4}{(E-E_{R})^2+\Gamma^2(E_{R})/4}$$which is valid in the vicinity of resonance.
