4-force is defined by
$$f^\mu=\frac{dp^\mu}{d\tau}$$
$$=\frac{d}{d\tau}\left( \frac{E}{c},\underline{p} \right)=\gamma \frac{d}{dt} \left( \frac{E}{c},\underline{p} \right)=\left( \frac{\gamma}{c} \frac{dE}{dt},\gamma \underline{F} \right).$$
We aim to describe how force transforms under a Lorentz transform![[Pasted image 20241026140951.png]]
$$F_{y}=\frac{dp_{y}}{dt}$$
$$F_{y}'=\frac{dp_{y}'}{dt'}=\frac{dp_{y}}{\gamma dt-\frac{\gamma \beta}{c}dx}=\frac{F_{y}}{\gamma\left( 1-\frac{\beta}{c}u_{x} \right)}$$
Similarly for $F_z$:
$$F_{z}=\frac{F_{z}}{\gamma\left( 1-\frac{\beta}{c}u_{x} \right)}$$
However for $F_{x}$, we get
$$F_{x}'=\frac{{F_{x}-\frac{\beta}{c}(\underline{F}\cdot \underline{u})}}{1-\frac{\beta}{c}u_{x}}.$$
From these we can see that in the rest frame:
$$F_{y}'=\frac{F_{y}}{\gamma};\;F_{z}'=\frac{F_{z}}{\gamma}$$
while
$$F_{x}'=F_{x}$$
