We can use the definition of [[4-Force]],
$$f^{\mu}=\frac{dp^{\mu}}{d\tau},$$
we can find an expression of the Lorentz force, $\underline{F}=q(\underline{E}+\underline{B}\times \underline{v})$, in tensor notation.
Using [[4-Momentum]],
$$p^{\mu}=\left( \frac{E}{c},\underline{p} \right)$$
$$f^{\mu}=\frac{dp^{\mu}}{d\tau}=\left( \frac{\gamma}{c} \frac{dE}{dt},\gamma \underline{F} \right).$$
Consider
$$F^{\mu \nu}u_{\nu}=(\gamma \underline{E}\cdot \underline{v},\gamma c[\underline{E}-\underline{B}\times \underline{v}]).$$
This gives a force of
$$f^{\mu}=\frac{q}{c}F^{\mu \nu}u_{\nu}.$$
